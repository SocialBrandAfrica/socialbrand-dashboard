About this view

Pulse Mini reads real Delareyville sales history from Pulse and turns it into a four-week order plan for each line. It groups lines by department, projects demand by mirroring the recent four-week pattern, compensates for weeks that look like out of stock, and shows margin, supplier and ordering position with the working on screen so every figure can be checked. Stock on hand is shown but not trusted, ordering runs off sales.

6-week real sales 4-week order plan Out-of-stock compensation Department grouping GP and capital, audited Live data with freshness
For sampling and demonstration only. The sales, costs and prices are real data from the live database, which already updates daily and feeds a working dashboard. The four-week projections are purely for illustration, they show how the data could drive ordering and are not a commitment or a final order figure. This view shows a small sample of lines to show what the data can do.
