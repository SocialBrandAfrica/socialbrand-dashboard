Why lines are picked (the badges): NEW negative = stock went below zero since it was last counted. Engine indictment = the Engine suspects the shelf and the ledger disagree. Inactive rotation = holding stock but not selling, presence unproven. Cyclical backbone = routine rotation so the whole store gets counted every cycle. Zero-SOH audit ⚑ = ledger says none, a spot check catches stock the system does not know it has. Deposits, production and non-stock lines never appear here, they have their own lists on the Fixer tab.
