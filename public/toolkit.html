Counting has one home. Lists are generated, printed, captured and exported in the stocktake app, where a store keeps its count and every step is logged with the user and the time. This page keeps the overview: how each store is counting and how much of its range the ledger has been shown to track.

The five measures, one definition each. Count pool: World 1 lines that sold or were received in 91 days, plus any World 1 line with stock not equal to 0. Counted in 91 days: a Sigma count posting (DIWAINV) inside 91 store days, over that pool. Normalised share: lines whose stock the ledger has been shown to track (two counts no more than 60 days apart and the later one within the tolerance), over the lines that sold in 91 days. Measured shrinkage: the counts on normalised lines after they qualified, in units and in rand at unit cost. Count compliance: daily lists generated in the app against lists finalised on time, on the Progress tab. The pool and the counted figure are the live read of the Platform Integrity tab on every tab (Progress, Platform Integrity and Stocktake); only the normalised share and the shrinkage are stocked with the nightly draw.
