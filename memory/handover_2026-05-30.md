## v_kpi_by_date vs mv_kpi_by_date — corrected understanding

v_kpi_by_date:  live GROUP BY aggregation over daily_snapshots (18M rows). Fast for 1–10 dates.
                Degrades exponentially with date-count — unusable for 90-date queries.
                Correct use: single-date KPI card (catches today's push immediately).

mv_kpi_by_date: pre-aggregated MV, 1 row per (store, date). 2,355 rows total.
                Refreshed by `Invoke-RefreshKpiView` at end of each store's nightly push.
                Always current to last push. Correct use: multi-date queries, trend (90 dates).

The trend fetch in page.jsx has always queried v_kpi_by_date with 90 dates — it has never
worked in production. The sales trend panel has never rendered for any user.
